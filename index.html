Two ways to compete

1-hour online hackathons. 3-hour in-person events. Big ideas in a short time.

Online and in person

Two formats. One community.

1 hour · Online

Compete from anywhere

Our 1-hour online hackathons are often standalone competitions. For major 3-hour in-person events, they can also serve as the qualifier, held two weeks before the main event to decide which teams advance.

3 hours · In person

Earn your place on stage

Build, pitch and compete in front of a live audience. With only a limited number of team spots available, online challenges or a 1-hour online competition determine who earns a place on stage.

Two weeks before: the selection round

All teams applying for an in-person event take part in an online challenge two weeks beforehand to determine who gets through. For our largest major events, this selection round takes the form of a 1-hour online hackathon. Successful teams advance to the 3-hour in-person event.
