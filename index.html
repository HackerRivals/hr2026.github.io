Live, physical and audience driven

What an event feels like

1
✉

Challenge shared

On the morning of the event the creative challenge is shared with all competing teams.

2
⌖

Teams arrive

Teams turn up physically at the event venue. This is a live competition, not a remote build.

3
⌨

Build room

Teams get a room to work in, with food and drinks provided while they build.

4
●

Round 1: Pitch

Each team gets five minutes on stage to pitch its idea to the audience.

5
ϟ

The Whammy

After Round 1 the audience voted Whammy is revealed. It adds a curve ball that teams must include in their solution.

6
▥

Round 2: Elimination

Each team gets five minutes again to show its idea, architecture and a demo if possible. The audience votes on the two finalists.

7
♜

Round 3: Final

The audience and judges vote on the winner. Cash and the trophy are awarded on stage immediately. No delays. No gift cards. Real cash.
