Two ways to compete

1-hour online hackathons. 3-hour in-person events. Big ideas in a short time.

Online and in person

Two formats. One community.

1 hour · Online

Compete from anywhere

Our 1-hour online hackathons are often standalone competitions. For major 3-hour in-person events, they can also serve as the qualifier, held one week before the main event to decide which teams advance.

3 hours · In person

Earn your place on stage

Build, pitch and compete in front of a live audience. Entry is selective: registering your team does not guarantee a place. Teams must qualify before joining the in-person competition.

One week before: the selection round

All teams applying for an in-person event take part in an online challenge one week beforehand to determine who gets through. For our largest major events, this selection round takes the form of a 1-hour online hackathon. Successful teams advance to the 3-hour in-person event.

The 3-hour in-person experience

What an event feels like

Once your team qualifies, here is what to expect on the day of the in-person event.

1
✉

Challenge shared

On the morning of the event the creative challenge is shared with all competing teams.

2
⌖

Teams arrive

Teams turn up physically at the event venue. This is a live competition, not a remote build.

3
⌨

Build room

Teams get a room to work in, with food and drinks provided while they build.

4
●

Round 1: Pitch

Each team gets five minutes on stage to pitch its idea to the audience.

5
ϟ

The Whammy

After Round 1 the audience voted Whammy is revealed. It adds a curve ball that teams must include in their solution.

6
▥

Round 2: Elimination

Each team gets five minutes again to show its idea, architecture and a demo if possible. The audience votes on the two finalists.

7
♜

Round 3: Final

The audience and judges vote on the winner. Cash and the trophy are awarded on stage immediately. No delays. No gift cards. Real cash.
